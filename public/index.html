Kiosk remote control

Stream the kiosk browser and drive it from here — use it for the one-time Planning Center login. The session cookie is saved in the kiosk's own browser profile, so it survives reboots.

Tap/drag the kiosk screen to click. Type in the bar to send keystrokes; Enter submits the form.
